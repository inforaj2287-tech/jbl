Privacy policy.

How JB Travels handles information shared through this website.

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, route details, travel date and other information you choose to provide. Basic technical information may also be recorded by the hosting service.

How we use information

We use enquiry and trip information to respond to you, coordinate a requested cab service, improve our website and maintain service records. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing and retention

We may share relevant trip details with a driver or service partner only when needed to coordinate your journey. We retain information for as long as reasonably required for customer service, accounting, safety and legal obligations.

Cookies and links

This website may use basic technical storage or cookies required for normal operation. External websites, including messaging and map services, have their own privacy policies and practices.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold, request a correction or raise a concern about its use. Contact contact@jbtravelsbelagavi.com or call +91-9740309999.
